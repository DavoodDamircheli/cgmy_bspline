the bad 1w accuracy. It is independent of, and uses a different time
horizon than, Cause A.

Neither (A) nor (B) explains the magnitude of the 1w error on any date:
Rannacher leaves the answer essentially unchanged (ratio ~1.0), and the
error does not refine at O(dt^2) even once the mesh clears the
steps_to_1wk>=1 threshold. The 'tau=0 err' column above shows why: the
error is already present in the L2-projected payoff BEFORE any time
stepping -- evaluation points for near-ATM strikes land within <1 grid
cell of the payoff kink (see 'distance from the kink' above), where the
smooth B-spline projection of the kinked payoff itself deviates sharply
from the true intrinsic value. This is a SPATIAL under-resolution bug
(scales ~O(h); halving h roughly halves it -- verified separately at
N=128/256), not a time-stepping bug -- consistent with the task's
'third bug' scenario for cause (B).
